This story map traces how the mix of crops grown across our Kansas study regions has changed over the
past century. Drawing on historical agricultural records and the memories shared in our oral
histories, it moves decade by decade — showing where the landscape grew more varied, where it grew
more specialized, and how those shifts lined up with changes in farms, markets, and communities.
