As our oral-history work gets going, we are grateful to the farmers, elders, and community leaders who
have already made time to talk with us.

Early conversations are surfacing the kinds of memories this project exists to preserve — how the mix
of crops in a region changed over a lifetime, how new equipment and shifting markets reshaped daily
work, and how the places people once gathered have changed along with the fields. People have been
generous, thoughtful, and often funny in describing lives lived close to the land.

We are recording these histories with permission and care, and we will share them here — as written
entries, audio, and story maps — as they are ready. If you would like to add your voice, the
[Participate](../../participate/) page explains how.
