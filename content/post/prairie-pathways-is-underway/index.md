We are glad to introduce **Prairie Pathways**, a community-centered research project based at Kansas
State University and supported by the U.S. National Science Foundation.

Over the past century, the crops grown across Kansas and the communities that farm them have changed a
great deal. Our project works to understand the socioecological processes behind that change — and to
document and celebrate the agricultural heritage of rural Kansas along the way.

In the months ahead we will be recording oral histories, building story maps, and visiting communities
across our study regions in North Central, Northwest, and South Central Kansas. This site will grow
into a home for that work — a place where the histories we gather can be explored, shared, and returned
to the communities they came from.

If you would like to take part or suggest someone we should talk with, please visit
[Participate](../../participate/). We would love to hear from you.
