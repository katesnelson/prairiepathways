  - block: markdown
    content:
      title: What taking part involves
      text: |
        - **A conversation, on your terms.** Interviews are semi-structured — more of a guided
          conversation than a questionnaire. They usually last about an hour and can take place in
          person, by phone, or by video, wherever is comfortable for you.
        - **You choose what to share.** You can skip any question, pause, or stop at any time.
        - **Recorded only with your permission.** With your consent we make an audio recording so your
          words are preserved accurately. You decide how your name and community are represented — and
          whether you would like a copy of your recording or transcript.
        - **Voluntary and unpaid.** Participation is entirely voluntary.

        Before any interview we walk through how the conversation will be used and answer any questions.
        Formal consent details are provided at that time in line with our university's research review
        procedures.
