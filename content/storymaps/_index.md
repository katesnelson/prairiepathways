Story maps weave together interviews, photographs, historical records, and geography into interactive
journeys you can scroll through and explore. They are a way to *see* how landscapes, livelihoods, and
communities have shifted over time — and to place individual stories on the map of the region they
come from.

The entries below are **illustrative samples** that show how finished story maps will appear on the
site. Each real story map will embed an interactive map (for example, built with Esri ArcGIS
StoryMaps or a similar tool) alongside narrative and archival imagery. As with our oral histories, we
describe locations at the regional level unless a contributor asks to identify their community.
