Where the companion story map follows the numbers, *Harvest & Heritage* follows the people. This
place-based tour celebrates the agricultural heritage of our study regions — the rhythm of the
seasons, the work of planting and harvest, the elevators and fairgrounds and kitchen tables, and the
knowledge passed down through families and neighbors.
