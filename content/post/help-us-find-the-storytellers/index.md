The heart of this project is the people who remember. We are looking to speak with farmers, ranchers,
community leaders, local historians, elders, and extension, conservation district, and USDA staff who
understand how their region has changed over time.

Introductions are just as valuable as interviews. If someone you know would be a good person to talk
with — a neighbor, a relative, a longtime member of a local historical society — we would be grateful
for the connection.

You can reach us any time through the [Participate](../../participate/) page. Thank you for helping us
preserve this heritage.
